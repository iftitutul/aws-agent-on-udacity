# Nimbus Market — Customer FAQ

Support phone: **+1 (888) 555-0142** — Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00 ET, Sat 10:00–18:00 ET.

---

## Orders

**How do I track my order?**
Sign in and go to Account → Orders → Track. A tracking email is sent within 24 hours of dispatch.

**Can I change or cancel an order?**
Orders can be cancelled from Account → Orders → Cancel within 60 minutes of being placed. After that the
order has entered fulfilment and cannot be changed; you can refuse delivery or return it once it arrives.

**My order says delivered but I did not receive it.**
Wait 24 hours (carriers sometimes scan early), check with neighbours and your building's parcel room, then
report the missing parcel from Account → Orders → Report a problem within 14 days of the delivery scan.

**Can I add an item to an order I already placed?**
No. Place a second order — orders shipping to the same address on the same day are combined automatically
when possible.

## Shipping

**What shipping options are available?**
Standard: 3–5 business days, $4.99, free on orders over $50.
Express: 1–2 business days, $12.99.
International: 7–14 business days, price calculated at checkout.

**Where do you ship?**
To 30 countries. Import duties and taxes are calculated and collected at checkout, so there is nothing to
pay on delivery.

**Do you ship to PO boxes?**
Standard shipping only. Express and international orders require a street address.

**When does the delivery window start?**
On the first business day after dispatch, not on the day the order is placed. Orders placed after 14:00 ET
are dispatched the next business day.

## Returns and Refunds

**What is the return window?**
30 days from delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

**How do I return something?**
Account → Orders → Return. Domestic returns include a prepaid label. International returns are at the
customer's expense.

**When do I get my money back?**
Refunds are issued to the original payment method 5–7 business days after the return arrives at our
warehouse.

**What cannot be returned?**
Gift cards, perishable goods, and opened personal-care items are final sale.

**Do you do exchanges?**
Not directly. Return the item for a refund and place a new order.

## Payments

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Nimbus Market gift cards.

**When am I charged?**
An authorisation hold is placed when the order is submitted; the card is charged when the order ships.

**My card was declined — why?**
The most common causes are a billing address that does not match the card, an expired card, or a bank
fraud block. Verify the billing details and contact your card issuer.

**Can I split a payment?**
One gift card plus one card can be combined on a single order. Multiple credit cards cannot.

**Do you store card numbers?**
No. Cards are tokenised by our payment processor; Nimbus Market never stores full card numbers.

## Products

**How do I know if an item is in stock?**
Stock status is shown on the product page. Sold-out items offer a restock alert by email.

**Do you price match?**
Yes, against listed prices at major retailers within 7 days of purchase. Submit the link from
Account → Orders → Price match.

**Is there a warranty?**
Electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Everything else is covered by the 30-day return policy.

## Account

**How do I reset my password?**
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes.

**Can I change the email on my account?**
Yes, from Account → Settings → Email. A confirmation link is sent to both the old and the new address.

**How do I delete my account?**
Email privacy@nimbusmarket.example from the address on the account. Deletion completes within 30 days.

**How long is my order history kept?**
Three years, then it is archived.

## Privacy

**What do you do with my data?**
Personal data is used to fulfil orders, prevent fraud, and — only with consent — to send marketing email.
Nimbus Market does not sell customer data.

**How do I opt out of marketing email?**
Account → Settings → Notifications, or the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

**How do I request a copy of my data?**
Email privacy@nimbusmarket.example. GDPR and CCPA requests are answered within 30 days.

**Do you use cookies?**
Yes — essential cookies always, analytics and advertising cookies only with consent, which can be changed
at any time from the cookie banner link in the footer.
