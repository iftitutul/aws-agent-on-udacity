# NovaMart Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return windows by customer tier
- **Standard members** may return eligible items within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- **Premium members** may return eligible items within **60 days** of the delivery date.
- The return window is calculated from the date the order was delivered, not the date it was placed.
- Orders that have not yet been delivered cannot be returned; they may be cancelled instead while in "Processing" status.

## Eligibility conditions
- Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals.
- Final-sale items, gift cards, digital downloads, and personalised products are not returnable.
- Opened software, perishable goods, and intimate apparel cannot be returned for hygiene and licensing reasons.
- An order that already has a return in progress (status "Return Initiated") cannot be returned again.

## Refund method and timing
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Once the return is initiated, customers receive a prepaid return label by email.
- Refunds are processed within **5-7 business days** after the returned item is received at our warehouse.
- Premium members receive **free return shipping**; Standard members pay a flat $5.99 return shipping fee that is deducted from the refund.

## Damaged or incorrect items
- Items that arrive damaged or incorrect can be returned at any time within 90 days, regardless of tier, with free return shipping.
