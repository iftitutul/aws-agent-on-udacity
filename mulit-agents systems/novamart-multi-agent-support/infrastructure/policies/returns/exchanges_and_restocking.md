# NovaMart Exchanges and Restocking Fees

## Exchanges
- Exchanges for a different size or colour of the same product follow the same window as returns: 30 days for Standard members, 60 days for Premium members.
- Exchanges ship free of charge once the original item is scanned by the carrier.

## Restocking fees
- Opened electronics returned within the window are subject to a 15% restocking fee for Standard members.
- Premium members are exempt from restocking fees on all returns.
- No restocking fee is charged for damaged, defective, or incorrectly shipped items.

## Late returns
- Returns requested after the tier window has closed are not eligible for a refund. Customers may still be eligible for warranty service if the product is defective.
