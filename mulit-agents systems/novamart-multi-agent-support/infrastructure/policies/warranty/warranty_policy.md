# NovaMart Warranty Policy

## Standard manufacturer warranty
- All electronics sold by NovaMart include a **1-year limited warranty** covering defects in materials and workmanship.
- Home goods and furniture include a **2-year limited warranty**.
- Apparel and accessories include a 90-day warranty against manufacturing defects.

## Premium member extended warranty
- Premium members automatically receive an **additional 1 year** of warranty coverage on all electronics (2 years total).

## What is not covered
- Accidental damage (drops, spills, cracked screens), normal wear and tear, cosmetic damage, unauthorised repairs or modifications, and damage caused by misuse.
- Consumable parts such as batteries after 6 months, unless the battery is defective on arrival.

## How to make a warranty claim
1. Contact NovaMart support with your order number and a description of the defect.
2. Support may request photos or a short video of the issue.
3. If approved, NovaMart will repair, replace, or issue store credit for the product at its discretion.
4. Warranty repairs are typically completed within 10-14 business days.

## NovaMart Protection Plan (optional)
- Customers may purchase a Protection Plan within 30 days of delivery. It adds accidental damage coverage for 2 or 3 years.
