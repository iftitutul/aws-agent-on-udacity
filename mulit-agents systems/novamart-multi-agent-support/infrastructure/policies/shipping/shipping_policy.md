# NovaMart Shipping Policy

## Shipping options and rates (continental US)
| Option | Delivery time | Standard members | Premium members |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Ground | 5-7 business days | $5.99, free on orders over $50 | Free |
| Expedited | 2-3 business days | $12.99 | $4.99 |
| Next-Day | 1 business day | $24.99 | $9.99 |

## Processing time
- Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern on a business day ship the same day.
- Orders placed after 2 PM or on weekends ship the next business day.

## Tracking
- A tracking number is emailed when the order ships. Order status moves from Processing → Shipped → Delivered.
- Customers can check order status at any time by providing their order number to NovaMart support.

## International shipping
- NovaMart ships to Canada and the United Kingdom. International delivery takes 7-14 business days.
- International shipping is a flat $19.99 for all members. Duties and taxes are the customer's responsibility.

## Lost or delayed packages
- If a package has not arrived 5 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support. NovaMart will open a carrier investigation and ship a replacement or issue a refund.
